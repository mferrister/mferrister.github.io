Professional profile

Understanding how sport works beyond the competition

My interest in sport has grown into a professional direction. I am particularly interested in the work that makes sports organisations and events function: administration, programme coordination, partnerships, stakeholder communication and the systems that support delivery.

Through CMD Sport Africa, my independent research and publishing project, I explore questions around African sport, business, governance, culture and commercial opportunity. I use writing and conversations with people in the industry to develop my thinking, test ideas and understand how the sector works.

Alongside this work, I volunteer with the Business of Sports Institute Africa (BOS Africa), have participated in the SIGAWomen Global Mentorship Programme, and completed the International Olympic Committee's Professional Sport Management and Sport Event Management courses.

I am not positioning myself as a sports journalist. My goal is to build a career in the administration, operations and partnership functions that help sports organisations, programmes and events do their work effectively.

Independent research & publishing

CMD Sport Africa

Research · Writing · Conversations

Exploring sport through an African lens

CMD Sport Africa is my independent platform for examining sport, business, culture and institutions from an African perspective. It is part research journal, part publishing project and part opportunity to learn from people working across the industry.

Through articles and conversations, I explore questions about how sport is organised, how commercial value is created, how events affect places and communities, and what opportunities exist for African sports organisations and professionals.
