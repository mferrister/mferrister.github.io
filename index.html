01 / About

Supporting the work. Understanding the bigger picture.

Based in Uganda, I work across administrative support, executive coordination and operations. I help leaders and teams manage priorities, organise complex workloads, coordinate people and information, and keep important work moving.

Alongside my administrative career, I am building a career in sport, with interests in sports administration, operations, partnerships and communications. Through this work, I am exploring how sports organisations operate, how relationships are built, and what it takes to turn ideas and ambitions into well-coordinated programmes and initiatives.

These two paths reflect a shared interest: understanding how organisations work and contributing to the systems, relationships and decisions that help them deliver. One gives me experience in supporting leaders and managing day-to-day operations; the other allows me to apply that interest within the business and wider ecosystem of sport.

Whether you are looking for executive support and operational coordination, or you are interested in connecting around sports business and development, I invite you to explore my work.
