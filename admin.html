About Me

With 2 years of direct experience built on a 5 year general background in NGOs, education, and remote work, I am making a deliberate move into long-term remote operations.

I always want to see the best outcome. Of course, the industry demands the best, but when you have a background in social work like I do and you strongly believe that systems, even when complex, can produce the best, you learn to seek out the best. Whether that means figuring out how best a team can survive, how best we present ourselves, or how best we can think and then get things done.

Sifting through complex information for hours does not bother me if it means a bookkeeping process can give us absolute clarity and transparency about the financial health of a company. I not only have to get it done, I want to, and I will.

I am here to act as a strategic partner for you. I am a partner who anticipates, plans, manages team expectations, and handles workloads, which is crucial when leading a team, especially a remote one. I manage competing priorities and close loops so that you do not have to. If you need someone committed to continuous learning, growth, and long-term commitment, then you do not have to look any further.

Take a look around; my experience, skills, and the work I've been doing are all here. I hope they give you a clear enough picture to want to reach out. I'd love to be part of what helps your team move.
