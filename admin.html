About me

How I approach the work

I work across administrative support, executive coordination and operations, helping leaders and teams bring structure to competing priorities, organise information, and keep important work moving.

My background spans NGOs, education, communications and administrative operations. My training in social work also shapes how I approach organisations: I pay attention to the people behind the processes, the systems they rely on, and the gaps that can prevent good work from getting done.

I enjoy work that requires both attention to detail and an understanding of the bigger picture. That might mean coordinating commitments, tracking decisions through to completion, improving how information is organised, or building a practical system that gives a team better visibility over its work.

I aim to be a dependable operational partner: someone who takes ownership of agreed responsibilities, communicates clearly, anticipates what needs attention, and follows through without requiring constant reminders.

This portfolio brings together my professional experience, selected work samples and practical demonstrations of how I approach administrative and operational challenges. I invite you to explore the work and get in touch if you think my skills could support your team.

Illustrative case study · Systems design

How Amara's Company Runs — Business Operating System

An illustrative case study based on a hypothetical 12-person fintech company working across Johannesburg, Nairobi, Lagos, London and Masaka. It explores a proposed operating system for organising financial records, documents and team coordination across locations.

The proposed architecture assigns distinct roles to three tools: Excel for financial tracking across five currencies, Google Drive for document organisation, and Notion for projects, tasks and operational coordination. The case study demonstrates a proposed design, not a system implemented for a real client.
